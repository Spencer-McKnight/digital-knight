### Method
Identify the journey methodology of digital partnership. Make this complex process of thought seem like a simple staged process with visual effects.
How do you find yourself in a new place on earth?
Dream → Fuel → Engine → Flight → Pioneer

**Dream:** Dare to dream. Set your goals. Where should you be? Who should you help? What do you want to achieve?

- I want to make so much money while I help people.
- I want to change the focus of my company.
- I want to understand and evolve with the time.
- I want absolute freedom from monotony.
- I want a digital presence that is a testament to my business.
- I want to show people the tool I forged with my love and sweat.

**Fuel:** The ideas, information and data. Brand assets, strategies, employees. Understanding that is power source of the next steps.

- The craziest ideas you have
- Your ideals for reliability and scale
- Your history
- Information about your customers
- Images and graphical elements
- Statistics and previous website analysis

**Engine:** Construct the thing that will move you forward. Design the dream invention, build it, and appreciate the new life that exists.

- Focusing
- Designing
- Building the digital tools
- Curating automation flows to reduce manual labour

**Flight:** Build the wings that will let you journey to new heights. Test flights, optimising and falling in love with your creation.

- Understanding analytics on your current digital applications
- Tweaking, optimising
- Celebrating and enjoying the initial successes

**Pioneer:** Explore where your business can go with this new ability.

- Looking towards the future
- Journeying to new places together
- Competing with others and winning
