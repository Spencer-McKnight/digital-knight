Success Criteria

public/images/propertyinvesting

Case Study:
Context
PropertyInvesting.com is a historical pillar of the property investing world. Created by Steve McKnight, it was one of the first idea entrants into the positive cashflow property world in Australia and America. After 20 years of successful business, their digital space was feeling resistant to change, was getting expensive, and had a problem with fake traffic clogging their servers.
Digital Knight was hired as a consultant to provide advice as to the future path forward for PropertyInvesting.com.

Success Criteria
Identify the current challenges and growth stagnation vectors
Suggest pathways to improve community growth and administrator experience
Reduce maintenance, server and hosting costs for the website
Provide design guidance as to future iterations

Outcomes
Digital Knight was able to identify the cause of extreme traffic, up to 97% of traffic was direct URL hits by bots. Similarly, these bots were posting much spam to the forum.
Additionally, we were able to suggest a path forward that would foster a 90% reduction in ongoing yearly costs.
Finally, a set of mockup designs were curated at no additional cost, leveraging intelligent AI prototyping, to guide conversation for future journeys for their digital experience to take.
The consultation work here and client satisfaction lead to our website rebuild for Steve McKnight's world-class fund, SOGIF.au

# Inlight.com.au

Building my expertise in modern development and agency agile

Case Study:
Context
Inlight is a Melbourne-based digital product agency that champions composable architecture, continuous evolution, and lasting digital value. They are not a project house. They build products that grow. Their client portfolio spans retail, automotive, food & beverage, health, government, travel and logistics. This was the environment in which Digital Knight's technical foundation was forged.

Before operating independently, Digital Knight spent two years as a Full Stack Developer at Inlight, absorbing the rhythms of agency delivery, building confidence across entirely new stacks, and watching real products ship to real users at scale. Every sprint was a lesson in how disciplined teams make complexity manageable.

Growth
At Inlight, the work was not theoretical. Each project came with strict deadlines, cross-functional collaboration, and the expectation to deliver polished digital experiences for high-profile clients. The skills carried forward today were built line-by-line across this body of work.

Vicinity's Chadstone Mobile App. Cultivated refined React Native componentry for the Chadstone shopping experience, contributing to a 59% increase in app downloads and the project winning the 2024 Australian Web Awards.

Nando's AU & NZ. Fostered a smooth year with zero rollback deployments across both web and React Native mobile feature releases. This was achieved through careful green/blue deployment design, AWS staging environments, and robust unit and integration testing.

Urban Rest. Created the check-in flow end-to-end, delivering a client-ready prototype in two weeks. Then led a small-team AI workshop to build a recommendation engine for accommodation matching.

AHM (Subcontract). Migrated their custom-built Sanity v2 CMS repository to Sanity v3, including a Node 14 to 20 upgrade and full package modernisation, all wrapped in careful risk management. This also provided exposure to larger-scale business processes, handling sensitive data security and compliance policies within a major health insurer.

Across all of these, Digital Knight built across the entire stack of multiple industry-leading digital experiences. Delivering hundreds of agile tickets in combination with strategy, design, leadership and testing. Performance and security optimisation strategies were planned and executed for multiple clients. Engaging presentations were delivered to clients with demonstrations and reflections, and also internally.

A type-generating, optimised, secure mediation layer between APIs and front-end was also built, bridging the gap between complex backend services and clean UI consumption.

Recognition
Digital Knight was awarded the Inlight Rising Star Award for accelerated growth and performance. A reflection of how quickly the agency environment can sharpen someone who arrives hungry to learn.

Reflection
Inlight taught Digital Knight how to ship. Not in isolation, but within the constraints of real business, real users, and real teams. The composable philosophy they practice, evolution over transformation, products over projects, became part of how every engagement is approached today.

The difference between building alone and building within a team of strategists, designers, delivery managers and engineers is perspective. Inlight provided that perspective, and the technical depth to back it up.
